Pet Licensing India

Mandatory municipal registration for your furry friends.

1

Locate Your Municipal Authority

Pet licensing in India is handled by local municipal corporations. Common ones include MCD (Delhi), BMC (Mumbai), BBMP (Bengaluru), and CCMC (Coimbatore).

2

Prepare Essential Documents

You will need digital copies of:

  • ✅ Aadhaar / Address Proof
  • ✅ Rabies Vaccination Certificate
  • ✅ Pet's Photo (Full Body)
  • ✅ Owner's ID Proof
3

Submit Application & Pay Fee

Apply online via the citizen portal or visit the local office. The fee typically ranges from ₹50 to ₹500 depending on your city.

4

Receive Your Metal Tag

Once approved, you'll get a registration certificate and a unique ID tag for your pet's collar. Remember to renew this annually!

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Fines for non-registration can range from ₹500 to ₹5000 depending on city bylaws.

🛡️

Why License?

A license ensures your pet is legally recognized, vaccinated for rabies, and helps authorities reunite you if they get lost.
